PROVEN TECHNIQUES

Durable Repairs
Built for Ontario Freeze-Thaw

Our team uses high-quality hot and cold-pour materials and proven compaction techniques to seal moisture out of cracks, smooth uneven surfaces, and extend the lifespan of your pavement by years.

Water trapped inside asphalt cracks expands when frozen during Canadian winters, rapidly breaking apart sub-bases into destructive potholes. Early crack sealing prevents catastrophic failure and saves thousands in full repaving costs.

Whether it’s small residential driveway patchwork or a full commercial parking lot surface refresh, MZ’s Lawn Care keeps your property looking clean, safe, and well-maintained!
